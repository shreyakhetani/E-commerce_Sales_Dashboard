# E-commerce_Sales_Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard built to analyze ecommerce sales data across Indian states, product categories, and customer segments.

> 🛠️ Built as part of my learning journey with Power BI Desktop.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Clothing dominates** – 63% of total quantity sold, making it the highest-volume category, followed by Electronics (21%) and Furniture (17%).
- **Maharashtra leads in revenue** – the top state by sales amount, followed by Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh.
- **COD is king** – 44% of orders are placed via Cash on Delivery, suggesting customer hesitancy toward digital payments.
- **Profit dips mid-year** – Months from June to November show negative or near-zero profit, indicating seasonal losses or high discount periods.
- **Printers are the most profitable sub-category**, followed by Bookcases and Saree.
- **Top customers** – Harivansh and Madhav are the highest spenders by order amount.

---
## 📊 Dashboard Visuals

- **KPI Cards** – Total Amount, Quantity, Profit, and AOV at a glance
- **Profit by Month** – Bar chart showing monthly profit trends with visible seasonal dip
- **Sales by State** – Horizontal bar chart of top states by revenue
- **Quantity by Category** – Donut chart showing category split
- **Quantity by Payment Mode** – Donut chart showing payment preferences
- **Profit by Sub-Category** – Horizontal bar chart of top 5 sub-categories
- **Amount by Customer** – Ranked bar chart of top customers
